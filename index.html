RAGDOLL RIOT

A physics shooter where everybody is a floppy ragdoll. Hold a building against zombies, or go after your friends.

PLAY NOW Free, in your browser. The Android app is in testing on Google Play.

Three ways to fight

Every hit throws bodies around with real physics, so no two fights go the same way.

Zombies

Hold the building together as the rounds get harder. Put boards back on the windows, buy better guns with your points and open new rooms.

Team Slayer

Red team against blue. The first team to the target score wins.

Slayer

Everyone for themselves. Add bots if you are short of people.

On a computer there is more: a free-play sandbox, solo modes, and extra team games including Gun Game, King of the Hill, Capture the Flag and Infection. Zombies has its own set of maps, two of them filling with lava as the rounds go on.

Play together

Up to 8 players in a room. Phones and computers can play in the same game.

  • Same Wi-Fi: host a game on a phone and everyone on your network sees it in their list.
  • Friends: every player gets a friend code. Give yours out and friends can join any game you host.
  • Anyone online: open your game to everybody, or join one that is already running.
  • On your own: fill the game with bots and play without anyone else.
  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No ads.
  • Your name, settings and progress are saved on your own device.
  • Playing online needs an internet connection.
